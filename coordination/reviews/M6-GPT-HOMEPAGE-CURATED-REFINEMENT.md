# Homepage visual review — 5 September 2026

The existing homepage was refined toward the coordinator's modern institutional academic direction. Its CMS-controlled composition and factual content remain intact. The requested redesign skills guided the audit, typography, density, and complete implementation; the user's explicit brief took precedence over randomized layouts, pinned scrolling, oversized spacing, synthetic assets, and decorative effects.

## Corrections verified

- Header: the committed implementation previously translated 52px for a 36px utility bar, clipping 16px from the main bar. The preserved correction translates only 36px. The logo uses `object-contain`, remains centered and fully visible, and the adjacent abbreviation is removed. Compact brand presentation through 1399px resolves longer EN/AR navigation overflow. Header composition, menus, badges, search and actions are retained. PPID text and PMB blue were darkened slightly for AAA contrast.
- Hero: the existing real group photograph and CMS focal point are retained; the text measure is widened, the desktop headline reaches 64px, and the overlay supports white text. No decorative badges or new content were introduced.
- Quick Access: six existing destinations form one horizontal strip on desktop and a compact two-column list on small screens. Existing external-link announcements are used.
- Dean: portrait and full message remain intact; a stronger section title and smaller serif quote improve image/text proportion.
- Statistics: Royal Blue remains unchanged. Numerals use tabular figures, aligned dividers, and existing reduced-motion-aware counting. White labels use a large bold treatment that meets enhanced contrast requirements on Royal Blue.
- About/programs: one tinted academic section, a restrained geometric motif, full-width program rows in IAT/IH/AFI order, and existing translated field descriptions replace narrow cards and unstructured empty space.
- News/ledgers: featured news remains dominant; supporting stories gain real thumbnails. Announcements and agenda form a compact divided ledger with existing dates and location information.
- Services: four equally legible service entries use functional icons, compact descriptions, separators, and a logical-direction Royal Blue interaction rule. Destinations remain unchanged.
- Facilities: the real photo mosaic retains its complete grid; height, crop, captions, and contrast are controlled.
- Partners: authentic full-color logos appear once, with natural aspect ratios and accessible links. Missing logos display the existing organization name.
- Video: the main poster fills its container correctly; supporting thumbnails form a compact adjacent index. Playback keeps the existing labeled YouTube privacy-enhanced player.
- Alumni: existing professional roles become the desktop selection index, paired with one focused testimonial. Small screens use compact name/year selectors. No outcome figures or quotations were invented.
- Academic highlights: one featured publication, three supporting articles, author/date metadata, and existing excerpt content establish the hierarchy.
- Lecturers: four curated profiles use CMS portraits first and verified existing leadership portraits as fallbacks for matching names. Roles and fields come from the current directory data. Mobile uses a keyboard-accessible portrait rail.
- Achievements: a single award now fills a composed image/story feature; additional awards retain compact supporting entries when available.
- Final CTA: Royal Blue anchors the closing section; PMB is a prominent white action, and the existing prospective-student destination remains present.
- Footer/map: one primary desktop grid, compact mobile grouping, high-contrast text, social targets of 44px, a 160px lazy map with title, original external fallback, and one slim legal row.
